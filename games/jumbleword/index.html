How to play

  • Tap the balls to spell a word, then press Enter. Tap a ball in your word, or the last ball you picked, to put it back.
  • Find every word hidden in the balls to fill the card. Each one is a row of dots until you find it.
  • A word that uses every ball is a House!
  • Bonus words are real words too unusual for the card, like QI or ROTA. They score too.
  • Whirl spins the dial to help you see new words.
  • On a keyboard, type letters, Enter to submit, Backspace to undo and Space to whirl.
